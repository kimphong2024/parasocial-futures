The question

How will AI reshape the fabric of our social relations by 2040?

Focused on the parasocial slice: companions, AI romance, grief tech, virtual beings, therapy bots — the places where a relationship with software starts doing the work a human bond used to do.

The instrument

Five windows over one evidence base

Signals→ Drivers→ Scenarios→ Artifacts→ Decision support

The binding rule across all five: nothing publishes without a human decision. Scan hits arrive pending; scenario drafts arrive as drafts; only explicit approval promotes them. The gate is the method, not a feature.

How signals arrive · nightly at 22:00 SGT

A candidate survives five steps to reach the review queue

  1. Undirected sweep — twelve themed questions to a search model, last-week recency
  2. Directed watch — sixteen configured sources, full articles read, never just headlines
  3. Classification + relevance gate — strict test: is the core subject AI's effect on human relationships?
  4. Deduplication — normalized URLs, then embedding similarity at 0.90 cosine
  5. Pending → human review — approve, reject, or edit; nothing enters the library by itself

Every knob is editable in the UI; every run records the exact settings and gate text it used.

The library

A library that refreshes itself

938approved signals
33clusters
579distinct sources

Seeded with the curated capstone corpus, extended nightly by the scan — every signal carries its cluster, type, horizon, provenance and the raw machine output it came from.

The library as one field

Clusters emerge — they are not drawn

Every approved signal, connected to its nearest semantic neighbours by embedding similarity and settled by a 3D physics simulation. Hovering a signal isolates its neighbourhood; dragging pins it against the field.

Drivers

Eight forces that move 2040

Recomposed from the library itself: each driver is a belief stated as a range — adoption, governance restrictiveness, prosocial design maturity, embodiment, legitimacy, displacement, harm accumulation, market concentration — justified by named signal clusters.
