# Futures of Parasocial AI

A foresight platform on how AI reshapes human social relations by 2040 — a University of Houston FORE 6397 capstone by Phong Huynh, live at parasocial-futures-production.up.railway.app.

## What it is

A human-in-the-loop foresight instrument: a 700+ signal scanning library refreshed nightly (Perplexity + Firecrawl + Claude classification), four CLA-structured scenarios over Dator archetypes, a Monte Carlo driver model, and a RAG decision-support chat. Nothing publishes without a human decision.

## Audience

- Public-policy makers working on AI governance
- Strategy and trust teams at AI companies (frontier labs, companion-app builders)
- Capstone reviewers and the foresight community

## Register

- `/` (home): **brand** — the page is the argument; a visitor should feel the instrument's seriousness in one scroll.
- All app pages (`/signals`, `/review`, `/scenarios`, `/simulation`, `/chat`, `/sources`): **product** — design serves the research work.

## Voice

Measured, literate, observational. Comfortable with uncertainty ("plausible", "emerging", "a weak signal suggests"). No hype, no exclamation marks, no emoji. The reader's intelligence is respected.
